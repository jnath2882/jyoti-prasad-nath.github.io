About

I turn complex technical information into clear documentation that people can act on: user and admin guides, deployment and upgrade procedures, API documentation, and maintenance manuals. I have 8 years of experience across telecommunications, enterprise software, and aerospace.

My start in aircraft maintenance engineering gives me something many technical writers don't have. I spent four years authoring Flight Crew Operating Manuals, Quick Reference Handbooks, and Minimum Equipment Lists at IndiGo, then compliance-driven maintenance documents at Air India Express. In that world a procedure has to be right the first time, because engineers, auditors, and regulators depend on it. I bring that same precision to enterprise software documentation.

Today I work in structured authoring with DITA/XML and CCMS platforms such as Adobe Experience Manager and SDL Tridion Docs, and I use AI tools like Gemini and Microsoft Copilot to speed up first drafts while keeping the review and accuracy standards high.

If you need a technical writer who can learn a complex product quickly, work closely with SMEs and developers, and ship accurate content on schedule, let's connect.
